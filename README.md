## Featured Project:

### CareSync — Smart Medication Management System

A multidisciplinary engineering project focused on developing an integrated medication management system, combining three interconnected components:

- **CareBox:** An automatic medication dispenser designed to support medication management.
- **CareBand:** A wearable device designed to complement the system.
- **CareApp:** A mobile application connecting the different components of the ecosystem.

**My role — Founder & Hardware Team Coordinator**

I conceived the initial idea, defined the overall system architecture and coordinated the hardware team throughout the project.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Leading and coordinating the hardware development team.
- Planning and distributing tasks while ensuring integration between development areas.
- Actively developing the CareBox, from initial design to prototyping and testing.
- Contributing to the electronic, mechanical and functional design of the system.
- Coordinating hardware, firmware and PCB development and validation.
- Ensuring compatibility and integration between the different subsystems.
